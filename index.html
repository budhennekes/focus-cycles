Plan · Focus · Review
Get clear on your outcome, what done looks like, and your first action. Set distractions aside while you focus. Review progress and distractions, choose your next action, then take a break.

Keep this tab open while focusing. Browsers can delay timers and sounds in background tabs or during sleep. When you return, the timer checks the saved deadline. Closing the tab stops sounds; reopening this same address restores saved progress.

History belongs to this browser, device, and website address. It does not sync with the Mac app or other devices. Clearing site data removes it. Use Export CSV to keep a readable copy of important notes. On browsers without tab locking, use only one Focus Cycles tab at a time.
