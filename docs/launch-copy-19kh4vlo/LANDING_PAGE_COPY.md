## Method
Heading: Give the work a beginning and an ending.

Plan the next step.
Name what you want to accomplish and how you'll begin. A small first action gives the session somewhere to go.

Stay with one target.
Your target stays beside the countdown. Pause when you need to, and take a break between cycles.

Keep the useful part.
Write down what happened. Save the reflection so the next session doesn't have to start from scratch.

## Mac conveniences
Heading: Keep the timer nearby, not in the way.
Use Compact mode beside your work or follow the countdown in the Mac menu bar. Move between Home, Session, and History without resetting your current session.

## Personalization
Heading: Make a little room to work.
Choose a scenic background or use your own image. Switch between light and dark appearances. Add local focus sounds, or leave the room quiet.

## Local records
Heading: Your session notes stay on your Mac.
Plans, reviews, and history are stored locally. Export your history as CSV when you want a separate record. The core timer and local sounds work offline; online scenery still needs a connection.
