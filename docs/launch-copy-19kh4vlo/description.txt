Sit down with a task, not just a timer.

Focus Cycles is a Mac focus timer built around a simple rhythm: plan the work, focus on one target, then review what happened.

Use it for a draft, a study session, or the work you keep putting off.

PLAN YOUR NEXT STEP
Choose your focus and break durations. Set a target, decide how to begin, and note what might pull you away. Start small with a single-cycle sprint or make room for a longer session.

STAY WITH ONE TARGET
Keep your target beside the countdown. Follow your progress, pause when you need to, and take a break between cycles. Use Compact mode or the Mac menu bar countdown when you want the timer nearby without the full window.

REVIEW WHAT HAPPENED
Record what you finished, what got in the way, and what to try next. Look back through saved sessions or export your history as a CSV file.

MAKE THE SPACE YOURS
Choose scenic backgrounds or your own photos. Switch between light and dark appearances. Add locally generated focus sounds, or work in silence.

ALSO INCLUDED
- A Home screen for daily intent and session setup
- Clear Home, Session, and History navigation
- Adjustable cycle counts, focus periods, and breaks
- Daily focus goals and optional quotes
- Keyboard shortcuts and session recovery

No account, ads, or subscription. Session plans, reviews, and history are stored on your Mac. The core timer and local sounds work offline; built-in online scenery needs an internet connection.

For Apple silicon Macs.
