# Focus Cycles Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

Focus Cycles is a local-first Mac app by Bud Hennekes. It does not require an account. The app does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or advertising identifiers. Online scenery contacts the third-party service described below.

## Data stored on your Mac

Session plans, reviews, debriefs, focus history, daily intent, settings, timer recovery state, and custom background images are stored locally. Focus Cycles does not upload this content to Bud Hennekes or an analytics service. Exported files go to the location you choose; sharing or syncing those files is controlled by you and your other software.

You can delete focus history in the app. For help removing all local app data, contact support. Deleting an app does not necessarily remove its data or copies in backups.

## Location

Weather is not included in this release. Focus Cycles does not perform IP geolocation or request weather data. Older preview versions may have saved weather coordinates in local settings. This release does not use or transmit those legacy values.

## Online scenery

Built-in online scenes load from images.unsplash.com. Requests can occur when the app opens or changes a scene. Unsplash receives your IP address and technical request information and may retain it under its policy: https://unsplash.com/privacy . Custom images are stored locally. You can select a gradient or your own image instead of online scenery; this does not undo requests already sent.

## Notifications

Timer notifications are local. Permission is requested when needed. Notification content is created on your Mac and is not sent to a Focus Cycles server.

## Support and your choices

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include are processed to answer your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or private session content. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information you shared with support, email bud@aboundlessworld.com. Third-party providers handle requests about their own records under their policies.

Focus Cycles does not sell personal data. It is a general productivity app, not directed to children under 13. Material changes to this policy will include an updated effective date.
